About

We build the tech — and the traction.

Zynique is a small studio focused on custom systems, websites, and marketing. We work with founders and small teams who need something built properly, without getting lost in a huge agency pipeline.

Every project starts with a real conversation about what you're trying to do. From there we design, build, and launch — whether that's a full internal system, a public site, or a campaign that finally makes your work visible.

If it can be coded, styled, or shipped, we probably enjoy doing it. And if it needs eyes on it, we know how to get those too.

What we do

Services built around your goals

Pick one, or roll them together. Everything below can flex to your stack, budget, and timeline.

Website Customization

Redesigns, rebuilds, and custom features on your existing site or from scratch. Fast, responsive, and easy for you to maintain.

  • Landing pages & full sites
  • Theme & template overhauls
  • Performance & SEO tune-ups

System Customization

Business systems shaped to how you actually work — not the other way around. Admin panels, dashboards, integrations, automations.

  • Custom dashboards & portals
  • Workflow automation
  • API & database integrations

Marketing Services

Content, socials, and campaigns that put your product in front of the right people. Practical, measurable, no fluff.

  • Social media & content
  • Brand & visual identity
  • Ad campaigns & funnels

Launch & Growth

End-to-end launches for new products or rebrands. We handle the site, the story, and the push — so day one actually feels like day one.

  • Launch campaigns
  • Analytics setup
  • Ongoing iteration
